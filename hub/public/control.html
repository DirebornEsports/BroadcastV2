Hero stats popup & GSI automation

Stats page

After the draft, each player takes a hero during strategy time. Dota then reports who is on which hero and the overlay fills the player name + picture into that card. Matching uses the Steam IDs from the Teams page. The dropdowns under the picks still work as a manual override.

When a game ends, the draft, winner and every player's hero, K/D/A and GPM are added to the stats. Remove test lobbies on the Stats page.

Countdown live

Belum jalan

OBS: Browser source /countdown 1920 × 1080 di atas scene starting soon. Kanan bawah = countdown ini, tengah = next match VS (diambil dari bracket, sama dengan kartu Next match di bawah).

Banner teks berputar

OBS: Browser source /banner 1920 × 1080 di scene in-game. Posisi dalam piksel layar 1920 × 1080; warnanya ikut desain turnamen.

Caster

Satu baris per frame caster: nama, lalu posisi plate nama di frame itu (piksel layar 1920 × 1080). Teksnya otomatis pas setinggi plate, dan tiap beberapa detik bergulir keluar-masuk per huruf. Tips: buka /caster?bg=1 untuk melihat kotak tiap plate.

OBS: Browser source /caster 1920 × 1080 di atas frame cam caster.

Karakter live chat (YouTube)

Tiap penonton yang ngetik di live chat YouTube dapat karakter kecil yang jalan-jalan di satu garis tanah, dengan nama dan chat-nya di balon. Yang diam 4 menit jalan keluar.

OBS: Browser source /chatwalk 1920 × 1080 di atas scene. Garis tanah dalam piksel layar 1920 × 1080; buka /chatwalk?bg=1 untuk melihat garisnya. Butuh secret YT_API_KEY (YouTube Data API v3) di Cloudflare; kuota gratisnya cukup ±6 jam baca chat per hari.

Next match overlay (countdown)

Schedule slips:

—

OBS: Browser source /nextmatch at 1080 × 1080 (square), or /nextmatch-wide at 1920 × 1080 (16:9 full screen, also shows the match after it). Both follow these settings. Logos come from the Teams page, the time from the bracket (Edit bracket). At zero it shows “Segera dimulai”, and “Live sekarang” when the series is marked live.

Running text (ticker)

OBS: Browser source /ticker at 1920 × 1080, on top of your scenes. Match info comes from the Next match settings above and the bracket. When the next match is marked live the badge turns red Live now.

Sound effects

Test buttons play through the draft overlay in OBS. Every OBS Browser source needs Control audio via OBS ticked so the sound reaches the mixer. Replace a sound by putting your own .mp3 with the same name in public/sounds/sfx/. Hero name calls (public/sounds/heroes) still play on picks.

Stats cards (full screen)

OBS: add a Browser source /statcards (1920 × 1080) on top of your scenes. Press a card to animate it in; press it again or Hide to take it off. Change the text and numbers in data/statcards.json; the next card you show uses the new version.
