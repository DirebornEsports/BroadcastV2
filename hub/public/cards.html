OBS: add a Browser source http://localhost:4747/statcards at 1920 × 1080, on top of your scenes. It stays invisible until you pick a card here.
Click a card to pop it in; click it again (or Hide) to take it off. Clicking another card swaps straight to it.
Keys: → next · ← prev · Esc hide.
Text and numbers live in data/statcards.json; edit, save, and the next time a card is shown it's updated. Press Reload cards to refresh the thumbnails.
